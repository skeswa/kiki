# D-NNNN: State the choice in plain language

- Recorded: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decided by: owner or agent, YYYY-MM-DD; source: the instruction or reasoning that decided it
- Topic: Name the topic
- Supersedes: —
- Superseded by: —

## Decision

What we will do and where the rule applies, in a few sentences a newcomer
can understand without opening another page.

## Why

The problem, evidence, and constraint that settled the choice. Mark unverified
assumptions and distinguish adoption from implementation or validation.

## Alternatives and consequences

Include only when another reasonable choice needs explaining: what else was
considered, why it was rejected, and what this choice costs. Otherwise delete
this section.

## Links

Link to the owning reference chapter, investigation, plan, or receipt. Add
more links only when they help the reader assess the choice.

Keep the record to about 40 lines. Put detailed evidence in an investigation.
Use the [decision process](README.md) to decide whether a record is needed.
