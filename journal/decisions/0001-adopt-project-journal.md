# D-0001: Separate current guidance, research history, and consequential decisions

Recorded: 2026-11-16  
Decided by: owner, 2026-11-16; source: “Please copy the journal and decision log concepts from swingset and replicate them here”; placement details adapted by the agent  
Topic: Documentation  
Supersedes: —  
Superseded by: —

## Decision

Use `journal/` for investigations, dated outcomes, numbered decisions,
research tools, evidence, and archived plans. Keep the reference book as the
current product contract. Record consequential choices briefly, identify the
actual decider, and link later reversals to the earlier record. Routine choices
remain in revision descriptions.

Index existing Herdr research, published probes, and book rationale at their
current locations. New journal entries use the new structure. Records have
no status workflow and add no approval step to already authorized work.

## Why

Swingset separates current guidance from evidence and the reasons behind
choices. Its current process narrows an earlier “record every decision” rule
to keep the log useful. Kiki needs that history as its terminal design evolves.

## Alternatives and consequences

Moving all existing material would unify paths but require changing published
URLs and research references. Indexing it preserves those links and original
evidence. New contributors must follow the journal indexes to older material.
Swingset's production policies and blanket delegation are outside this adoption.
