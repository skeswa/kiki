# Decisions

This chapter keeps short design notes that explain why the contract took its current shape.

New consequential choices go in the repository's [numbered decision log](https://github.com/skeswa/kiki/blob/main/journal/decisions/README.md). A consequential choice is one that changes what kiki does or how it is built.

The notes in this chapter are older than that log:

- They keep their published locations.
- They are indexed in the decision log without invented dates or deciders.
- A later record can supersede (replace) a note or a specific part of it, while updating the affected contract chapters.

These notes are part of the book, but they are not a higher authority than the behavioral chapters. If a decision note and a normative chapter (a chapter that sets rules) appear to disagree, use the authority order in [the book index](../../).
